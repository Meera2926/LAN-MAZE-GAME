# LAN Maze Game - Achievement System Guide

## Milestones & Rewards
1. **FIRST_WIN**: Reach the escape portal before your opponents or bot adversaries.
2. **COIN_HOARDER**: Collect over 50 gold coins in a single match.
3. **SPEED_DEMON**: Escape the labyrinth in under 45 seconds.
4. **TRAP_DODGER**: Clear a game without triggering any spike or freeze traps.
5. **BOT_HUNTER**: Use a Freeze power-up on an active bot within 5 tiles of the exit.

## Tracking
Achievements are recorded locally and broadcast via `StatsPacket` at the end of each round.
