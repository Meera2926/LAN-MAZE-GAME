# Game Mechanics & Power-Up System Specification

## Collectibles & Scoring
- **Gold Coin**: +10 pts (Multipliers active with consecutive streak).
- **Coin Bag**: +50 pts (Spawns at dead ends to encourage exploration).

## Power-Up Balancing
- **Speed Boost**: 2x player velocity for 8 seconds.
- **Ghost Walk**: Phasing through interior maze walls for 5 seconds.
- **Radar Reveal**: Disables fog-of-war revealing nearby competitors for 10 seconds.
